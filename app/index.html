Kenya Pulse

A dipstick on the Kenyan economy, and a read on where money is being paid.

Thirty-one published indicators from free sources, in four layers of signal: where money is actually being paid, after tax and inflation; what is building before it prints; what is coming but has not printed; and which long-held relationships have come apart. No login, no paid feed and no composite score.

Built and published by Brian Gachichio. Free to open and installs like an app. The code is open on GitHub.
